5 hero layouts

Mockups based on the "Stop Designing Boring Hero Sections" reference — same portfolio content, five different layouts.

01

Staircase

Dark tech style. Blocks step down toward a centred headline; projects stack as descending steps.

 02

Bento Mosaic

Monochrome grid of mismatched tiles — headline, portrait, stats and CTA each in their own cell.

 03

Floating Showcase

One hero object floating centre stage on a soft pink gradient, with info cards pinned to the corners.

 04

Diagonal Split

Editorial serif headline on the left, image panel cut on a diagonal; projects alternate the cut.

 05

Arc Glow

Huge glowing arc over a centred wordmark on near-black — a product-launch feel.
